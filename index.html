Pact Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Pact is developed and provided by Individual Entrepreneur Karetkin, based in Kazakhstan ("the developer", "we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information we process as described in this policy. This policy describes the current free version of the Pact iPhone app and our handling of related support requests. It also explains what happens when you visit this policy on GitHub Pages. It applies wherever Pact is available.

For app support and privacy requests, contact pact.app@protonmail.com. You can also contact the developer directly at karetkin.danil@gmail.com.

2. Information processed on your device

Pact does not require a user account. The current free app does not include advertisements, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not upload your Screen Time activity or blocking choices to a server operated by the developer.

Pact processes the following information on your device to provide the features you choose:

  • Blocking choices: apps, categories, and websites you select through Apple's Screen Time picker, website addresses you enter manually, and your always-allowed selections. Apple's picker uses privacy-preserving selection tokens.
  • Rules and protection state: names and settings of your schedules and daily time limits, their enabled states, information indicating whether a daily allowance has been reached, and any deadline for automatically resuming protection after you pause it. This information lets Pact and its extensions apply and remove restrictions.
  • Usage reports: Screen Time activity supplied by Apple is processed within Pact's Screen Time report extension to display usage summaries and time projections. Pact does not export those reports to the developer or to the main app's shared storage.
  • Setup and access preferences: onboarding status and local markers recording your lifetime early access and acknowledgement of the early-access message. These markers do not contain your email address or Apple Account credentials.

3. Screen Time permission and your control

Screen Time authorization is required to use Pact so it can display usage reports and apply the restrictions you choose. You can decline this permission; Pact then stays on the permission screen without access to setup or the main app.

You can turn protection off from Home or Settings, either for 15, 30, 60, or 120 minutes or indefinitely until you turn it back on. A timed pause stores a deadline on your device so protection can resume automatically. Your saved rules and reached daily allowances are kept during a pause.

You can revoke Pact's Screen Time authorization in iOS Settings. Revoking authorization returns Pact to the permission screen while preserving your saved configuration and setup progress. It does not delete Apple's Screen Time history. Granting access again lets you continue setup or return to the main app.

4. Local storage, backups, and deletion

Your saved configuration remains on your device until you change it, reset your setup, or delete the app. Pact shares the protection state needed for background enforcement with its own monitor extension through an on-device app group. That protection-state directory is excluded from device backups. Other app preferences may be included in backups managed by iOS, iCloud, or your computer, depending on your settings; the developer does not receive those backups.

To clear your saved blocking choices and stop Pact's restrictions, open Settings → Start setup again → Reset pact and stop protection. Reset preserves the local markers for lifetime early access and acknowledgement so you do not lose that access or have to acknowledge the message again.

Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data through iOS. Existing backups, Apple's Screen Time records, and correspondence you sent to support are managed separately. Manage device backups and Screen Time records through Apple's settings and services; contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. The developer cannot remotely access or erase data that stays only on your device.

5. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, any name included with the message, its contents, attachments, and ordinary email metadata. This may include device details, screenshots, or logs you choose to send. Please send only information relevant to your request.

We use this information to respond, investigate problems, handle privacy requests, and follow up on the issue. Sending an email is optional and is not required to use Pact. We do not use support correspondence for advertising or mailing lists.

Support correspondence is retained for up to 12 months after the request is resolved, to handle related follow-up and recurring issues, unless a longer period is required by applicable law or necessary for a specific unresolved legal claim. We delete it earlier when it is no longer needed or applicable law requires deletion. You can request earlier deletion. Copies in the email provider's backups may remain subject to that provider's retention procedures.

Messages sent to the app support address are handled through Proton Mail. Messages sent to the developer's Gmail address are handled through Gmail. These providers process email under their respective terms and privacy policies: Proton Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

The developer handles support through email. We do not use a separate helpdesk, customer relationship management platform, or AI service to process your correspondence.

6. Apple services and diagnostics

Apple operates iOS, Screen Time, the App Store, and any Apple backup or diagnostic services you use. Depending on your Apple settings, Apple may collect diagnostics and make crash reports or app statistics available to developers. We may use information Apple makes available to investigate crashes and improve reliability. Pact does not include a separate third-party crash-reporting SDK.

Apple's processing is described in its Privacy Policy. You can review diagnostic-sharing options in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

The current version covered by this policy is free and does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. We will update this policy before introducing changes that affect how personal information is processed.

7. Sharing and external services

We do not sell or rent your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information you send to support is processed using the email services described above. We may disclose information we hold when legally required, or where necessary and lawful to address a security incident or establish, exercise, or defend a legal claim.

The optional sharing feature opens Apple's share sheet. You choose whether to send an invitation and which app or recipient receives it. Sharing is never required to use Pact or keep your lifetime early access. Pact does not upload your contacts or automatically include your Screen Time reports or blocking selections in the invitation. The app or service you choose handles the shared message under its own privacy practices.

9. International users and processing grounds

The developer operates in Kazakhstan and handles support correspondence there. Apple, our email providers, and GitHub may process information in other countries under their respective privacy policies. The on-device Screen Time information described above is not uploaded to the developer merely because you use Pact from another country.

Where the European Economic Area or United Kingdom data protection rules apply, we rely on the following grounds to the extent they apply to the processing concerned:

  • Providing the service you request: processing necessary to provide the Pact features and related support you request under our agreement with you.
  • Legitimate interests: handling general enquiries, following up on technical issues, protecting the service, and investigating reliability problems, where those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Legal obligations: responding to legally required requests and keeping records where applicable law requires us to do so.
  • Consent: where applicable law requires consent for particular processing, we obtain it before that processing. You may withdraw it as described below. Granting an iOS permission is not blanket consent to unrelated data processing.

International processing remains subject to applicable transfer requirements. Where a transfer requires a specific safeguard or authorization, it must be in place before that transfer. Contact us for information about the arrangements applicable to your information.

10. Privacy requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to complain to a competent data protection authority. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Send requests to pact.app@protonmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify that the request relates to you. These requests concern information the developer actually holds, such as support emails; we cannot retrieve your on-device Screen Time activity.

We respond within the period required by applicable law. We do not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights. Where you have a right to appeal our response, you can request a review by replying to our email. You may also contact the competent privacy regulator in your jurisdiction.

11. General audience and children

Pact is intended for a general audience, including younger users where permitted by applicable law and Apple's platform rules. We do not impose an adults-only restriction. App Store age ratings, device settings, and Screen Time authorization requirements still apply.

Pact does not require users to provide their name, email address, or date of birth to use the app. Its Screen Time reports and blocking choices are processed on the device as described above. Parents or guardians can contact us on a child's behalf about support or privacy matters.

If we learn that a child has provided personal information to us that we are not permitted to retain, we will delete it or obtain the authorization required by applicable law before continuing to process it. Please contact our support address to raise a concern or request deletion of a child's correspondence.
